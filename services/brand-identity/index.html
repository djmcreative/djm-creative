Your brand is everything a customer associates with you.

It's your logo, your colors, your voice — but it's also the way your emails sound, the way your website feels, and the gut reaction someone has when they hear your name. A strong brand earns trust faster, charges higher prices, and gets recommended more often.

We build brand identities for Twin Cities businesses that want to look as good as they actually are. No mood boards full of clichés. No identities that could belong to anyone. Real strategy, then design that follows.

Who this is for.

Brand identity work isn't for everyone. It's most valuable in these moments.

  • New businesses
    Starting fresh and want a strong foundation instead of duct-taping a brand together later.
  • Rebrands
    Your current identity has outgrown your business — or never quite fit in the first place.
  • Expansion
    Going beyond a local market and need a brand that can travel without losing its edge.
  • Premium positioning
    Trying to charge more. Price perception is heavily influenced by how you look.
  • Acquisitions
    Just bought a business or absorbed a brand and need to signal what's new.
  • Internal alignment
    Teams working off different logos, decks, and voice guidelines. A unified system fixes that.

How a brand project runs.

Four phases, roughly six weeks start to finish.

  1. 1
    Discovery. Deep questionnaire, competitor research, customer interviews if relevant. We dig into who you are and who you're for.
  2. 2
    Strategy. Positioning document, messaging framework, creative direction. The thinking before the designing.
  3. 3
    Design. Logo concepts, refinement, visual system, applications. Real iteration, not "pick from these three options."
  4. 4
    Delivery. Final files, brand guidelines, templates, and a handoff call so you actually know how to use everything.
