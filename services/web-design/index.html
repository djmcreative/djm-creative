Platforms we build on.

We pick the right platform for your needs, not whichever one we feel like using.

  • Webflow
    Best for design-forward marketing sites that need to be visually distinctive and easy to edit.
  • WordPress
    Best for content-heavy sites and businesses that need a real CMS with plugins.
  • Shopify
    Best for e-commerce. Period. Including your merch store.
  • Static sites
    Best for fast, simple, secure marketing pages where you don't need a backend.
  • Framer
    Best for landing pages with sophisticated animations and quick iteration.
  • Custom-coded
    Best for projects with unique functionality that doesn't fit any off-the-shelf platform.

How a web build runs.

Five phases from kickoff to launch, typically 6–10 weeks.

  1. 1
    Discovery & sitemap. Audit your current site (if any), understand your goals, plan the pages and user flow.
  2. 2
    Design. Homepage design first for direction approval, then the rest of the pages. Figma mockups, real iteration.
  3. 3
    Build. Development on the chosen platform. Built mobile-first, pixel-accurate to designs, with SEO baked in.
  4. 4
    Content & QA. Final content load, cross-browser testing, performance audit, analytics setup.
  5. 5
    Launch. DNS switch, go-live checklist, post-launch monitoring. Training if you're managing it yourself.
