Search has changed. Your strategy should too.

For two decades, SEO meant one thing: ranking on Google. Today, your customers are also searching in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude — asking AI assistants for recommendations and getting answers without ever clicking through to a website.

We build SEO strategies for both. Traditional Google rankings and the new generation of AI search engines (sometimes called GEO, or Generative Engine Optimization). Because if your business doesn't show up in either, you're invisible.

If a customer asks ChatGPT to recommend a Minneapolis bakery and you don't show up, that's a lead you'll never even know you lost.

What's included.

Every SEO engagement is tailored to your business. Most include a mix of these core deliverables.

Technical SEO Audit

Site speed, mobile experience, crawlability, schema markup, indexing — the foundation everything else depends on.

Keyword & Topic Research

What your customers are actually searching for, and where the realistic ranking opportunities are.

On-Page Optimization

Titles, meta descriptions, headings, internal linking, content structure — the on-page levers that move rankings.

Local SEO

Google Business Profile optimization, local citations, review strategy, and Twin Cities-specific ranking tactics.

Content Strategy

The long-form content that earns rankings, builds authority, and gets cited by AI search engines.

AI Search Optimization

Structured data, conversational content, citation-worthy formatting — the signals AI search relies on.

What the first 6 months look like.

SEO is a long game. Here's a realistic timeline.

  1. 1
    Month 1 — Audit & strategy. Technical audit, keyword research, competitor analysis. We build the plan and tackle the quick technical wins.
  2. 2
    Months 2–3 — Foundation. On-page optimization, local SEO setup, schema markup, and the first wave of content. You start seeing movement on less competitive queries.
  3. 3
    Months 4–6 — Compounding. Content library grows, authority builds, local signals strengthen. Rankings improve on more competitive terms. Organic traffic starts to actually matter.
  4. 4
    Month 6+ — Flywheel. Content begets links. Links beget rankings. Rankings beget traffic. Traffic begets customers. The system starts running itself.
  5. 5
    Ongoing. Monthly reporting, continuous optimization, new content, AI search monitoring. We don't set it and forget it.
